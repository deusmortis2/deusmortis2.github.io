ASTRO STOMP

Move: arrow keys or A/D   Jump: space, up or W   Smash: down or S while in the air   Fire: X or J (with a blaster)   Fullscreen: F
Fly out from Mercury past Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn and Uranus to Neptune. Each planet has four kinds of alien with their own tricks (hot shells, poison gas, kicked turtle shells, burrowing scorpions, gusts, gravity wells, freezing frost and mind rings that swap left and right), and a boss guards the rocket at the end. A boss can only be smashed when it's worn out after a big attack: watch for SMASH! Beaten aliens sometimes drop power-ups: shields, blasters, jet boots, speed, star power, quake smashes, oxygen and extra lives. You get 3 lives; lose them all and your score resets to 0, but you go back to the last checkpoint flag (or the boss's door). Your oxygen meter drains fast: only O2 tanks refill it, and it stops draining during the boss fight. Every 30 seconds you get a boost: bonus points and a short shield. The faster you clear a planet, the bigger the bonus when you board the rocket. Press C on the title screen to carry on from the furthest planet you've reached.
