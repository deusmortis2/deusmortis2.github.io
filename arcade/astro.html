ASTRO STOMP

Move: arrow keys or A/D   Jump: space, up or W   Smash: down or S while in the air   Fullscreen: F
Cross Mars, Venus and Saturn and reach the rocket on each. Smash aliens from above; touching them, their shots or a hazard costs a life. Every alien has a trick: stalkers pounce, blades throw discs, brutes take three smashes and send out shock waves (jump them), ravagers charge and get dazed if they hit a wall, brains fire orbs and flies dive or drip acid. Watch out for cliffs, low cave roofs, falling rocks, crushers, lava geysers and lasers. You get 3 lives for the whole trip. Your oxygen meter drains fast: grab O2 tanks to refill it (they also give back a lost life), and once it's empty you lose a life every 2 seconds. Lose all 3 and the mission starts over. Every 30 seconds you get a boost: full oxygen and a short shield.
