DEUS DRACONIS

Move: arrow keys, WASD, or drag   Fire: space or tap   Power-ups: click a card or press 1 to 3   Fullscreen: F
Survive three 60-second stages: the blue sky, the night sky, then low over the countryside. Each stage ends with a wyrm boss. Every monster attacks in its own way, so watch for warning lines and flashes. Kills level your dragon up for faster fireball recharge, and from level 5 you pick a new power every few levels. You get 3 lives for the whole flight. Halfway through each stage, you get a power boost: a short shield, +10 points and full fire.
